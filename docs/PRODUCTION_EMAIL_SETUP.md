## Ordered implementation and owner handoffs

1. Obtain explicit approval for the exact domain, plan and costs. Recheck the
   quote immediately before commitment. No purchase or paid trial activation is
   authorised solely by the budget ceiling. Domain availability is not trademark
   clearance; complete the working-name check before a launch.
2. The owner reviews/enters registrant, billing and payment details privately.
   Registry/registrar disclosure rules apply; WHOIS redaction is not a guarantee
   of anonymity. Do not put identity, tax, card or recovery data into Git or chat.
   Record the actual registration and renewal total before purchase.
3. Start the one-user business signup using the owned domain. The owner completes
   credential entry, authentication and any agreement acceptance. Verify the
   **Business Starter / Flexible** selection; no annual commitment or paid add-ons.
   Retain the applicable business agreement, contracting entity, CDPA and invoice
   privately. A consumer Gmail subscription/label change is not evidence that the
   new business arrangement covers this mailbox.
4. Verify domain ownership and configure mail routing using **the exact records
   generated for the account**, not guessed TXT tokens, MX or DKIM keys. Owner
   handles domain-access, authentication/security and privacy settings. Prepare
   the record list for review and check propagation afterward. Avoid duplicate
   SPF records; review existing DNS before adding records. Use the provider's
   SPF/DKIM/DMARC guidance and verify received headers, not just UI success.
5. Owner enables/verifies strong authentication and recovery, reviews access and
   optional AI/smart-feature/third-party settings, and accepts any required data
   terms. No email forwarding or assistant access to real customer messages is
   implied. Do not disable protections to resolve setup problems.
6. Apply the [support/privacy operating procedure](SUPPORT_PRIVACY_OPERATIONS.md)
   after approval. Check actual provider roles, processing locations, transfer
   terms, deletion limits and supplier access. Workspace Starter is **not an
   EEA-only-processing promise**. Business mail does not settle the separate
   Cloudflare and Lemon Squeezy questions.
7. Test inbound mail from an owner-controlled external mailbox and a reply from
   the new support address, then the privacy alias. Check Inbox/Spam, correct
   From/Reply-To and readable signature, plus received SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
   Sending each external test requires an explicit destination/authorisation;
   do not invent an address or contact prospective customers.
8. Only after passing tests, update current website/contact/privacy copy and
   provider support/reply-to settings through their normal review flow. Keep
   checkout in Test mode. Record exact public text, provider terms and the test
   result. Releasing paid ZIP terms and enabling sales are separate approvals.
