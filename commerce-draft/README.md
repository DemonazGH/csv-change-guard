# Product-page and checkout drafts

These are local, static review pages, updated on 22 October 2026. They are not
a public offer, payment integration, entitlement system, or deployed website.
Every page is marked **Draft — not for sale**. Payment and download controls are
disabled. Following a link never simulates an order or grants a license.

Open `index.html` directly in a browser, or, from the repository root, preview
the directory with a local development command:

```powershell
python -m http.server 8091 --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory commerce-draft
```

Open `http://127.0.0.1:8091/`; stop the server with Ctrl+C. Python is only a
convenient preview tool. The draft pages and the app do not require Python to
run. Do not disable browser sandboxing or device protections to preview them.

| File | Review purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `index.html` | Product promise, target customer, scope, limits, proposed offer and FAQ |
| `checkout.html` | Proposed purchase summary, fit disclosure, draft commercial/support/privacy/refund terms |
| `confirmation.html` | Sample delivery instructions and cancellation, pending-payment, missing-download and device-blocking states |
| `styles.css` | Shared responsive styles; no imported fonts or assets |

The proposed offer is **$29 USD once, plus applicable tax finalized by the
payment provider**, for a downloadable utility licensed to one business and its
staff and contractors working solely on that business's own catalogs, across
its owned stores and devices. Ordinary backups are allowed. Agency/multiple-client
services for separate businesses, resale, sublicensing, and redistribution are excluded. Unlimited local
comparisons remain subject to the app's input limits.

The proposed offer allows continued use of the delivered version and includes
12 months from purchase of email support and maintenance updates when released
through the original delivery channel. There is no automatic renewal or updater;
future major versions and perpetual support are not promised. Mandatory rights
and required updates are unaffected. There is no subscription or per-report gate.

Support contact: **csvchangeguard.support@gmail.com**. The proposed first-response
target is two business days, Monday–Friday excluding Polish public holidays,
during the proposed 12-month support period. This is not a resolution guarantee.
Start with error text and synthetic examples; do not send merchant CSVs, reports,
credentials, or payment details. Managed comparisons and live-store access are
outside the beta.

The proposed voluntary policy is a full refund requested within **14 calendar
days of purchase**, without a reason, including after download. Send the order
number and purchase email to support; refunds would be handled through Lemon
Squeezy, with provider/bank timing outside our control. After a completed full
refund, stop using and delete utility copies, including backups; the buyer's
own generated reports may be kept. Local copies cannot be remotely revoked.
Mandatory rights are separate and are not shortened or waived.

The owner approved these commercial defaults on 22 October 2026; final contract
and legal review remain pending. They are not effective terms or a license grant. The legal seller/licensor identity, public service address,
required registration details, effective release and terms date remain placeholders.

These pages have no JavaScript, purchase/contact forms, external resources,
analytics, tracking, CSV selectors, or network integration. Their CSP disables
connections and form submission. All styles and navigation are local. The
application's local-processing behavior is unchanged. Lemon Squeezy purchase
data and email support are separate flows; neither requires catalog files.
Final purchase/support privacy disclosures and provider roles need review.

succeeded on **22 October 2026**, using an official fake card. The internal ZIP

actual hosted delivery remains unverified. No real payment was taken and live
sales remain disabled. This is distinct from the earlier Windows Chrome/Edge
application checks and does not establish paid demand.

The local checkout layout previews copy and information order; it is not a
replica or promise about the provider's interface. These pages contain no
checkout URL, provider integration, order identifiers, or private delivery links.
Pending, cancellation, duplicate submission, delivery, refund, and recovery flows
remain separate checks. A static confirmation URL is never proof of payment.
