Review what you would receive.

A downloadable utility for local, read-only comparison of a supported Shopify product-CSV subset.

In the proposed beta package

  • A standalone HTML app for local comparisons; no CSV uploads or store credentials.
  • Synthetic sample files and a built-in demo to learn the workflow.
  • A quick start, field-coverage guide, and troubleshooting help.
  • HTML, CSV, and JSON report exports; unlimited comparisons within the documented limits.
  • A proposed one-business commercial license, including staff using it for that business, with beta updates.

Check the fit first

Both files must be UTF-8, comma-delimited CSV from the same store and currency, with recognized handle, Title, and SKU columns. Matching uses exact, case-sensitive handle + non-empty SKU pairs.

Limits per file: 5,000 data records, 10 MiB, and 200 columns. Prices use the documented non-negative dot-decimal format.

Images, inventory, shipping, taxes, markets, metafields, and full importer behavior are unassessed. Missing or ambiguous identifiers mean incomplete comparison. Absent rows do not predict deletions; omitted columns differ from explicit blanks.

A report does not approve an import or guarantee safety. This utility does not perform imports or change a store.
