CSV CHANGE GUARD — LOCAL VALIDATION KIT
Prototype 0.1.0 — no account, store connection, or payment required

1. Download the ZIP and extract all files into a folder on your computer.
   Do not open the app inside the ZIP preview.

2. Open the dist folder. Right-click index.html, choose Open with, then
   Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. No installation or build is needed.

3. Click "Try the built-in demo". You should see:
   Compared variants: 4       Price changes: 4
   Priority findings: 6       Unpaired update rows: 4
   All findings: 15
   An incomplete-comparison verdict is expected for this deliberately risky demo.

4. Click "Save HTML report". Open the downloaded report in your browser.
   Confirm that you can understand the findings without using the app.

5. Click "Clear files". For a file-selection test, choose these included files:
   Current catalog: samples/current-catalog.csv
   Proposed update: samples/proposed-update.csv
   Leave the threshold at 30% and overwrite enabled. Click "Compare files".
   The counts should match step 3.

More scenarios and expected results: samples/scenarios/README.md
Full instructions and later real-data validation: docs/LOCAL_VALIDATION.md
Copy the results sheet for your notes: docs/VALIDATION_RESULTS_TEMPLATE.md

All supplied catalogs are synthetic. Do not import them into a real store.
The app only compares files; it never performs an import or modifies a store.
No findings does not mean an import is approved. Images, inventory, taxes,
shipping, markets, and metafields are outside the comparison.

If your browser blocks local HTML, do not change its security settings.
If Node.js is already available, open a terminal in this extracted folder,
run "node server.cjs", and open the local address printed by the command.
Keep the terminal open while testing; press Ctrl+C to stop afterwards.
Otherwise record the blocked launch so we can help with a supported option.

For feedback on these synthetic tests, share the browser, the four counts,
whether the report opens, and any confusing step. You do not need to send CSV
files, screenshots, or exported reports. Keep any later real store files and
reports on your computer, outside the source repository and AI workspace.
