    Coverage, matching rules and limitations

      What to take from the example

      Useful findings.
      Human decisions.

      01

      Excluded means incomplete.

      The proposed update repeats notebook / NOTE-001. The engine cannot choose a reliable match, so it excludes those rows instead of guessing a price comparison.

      02

      Different kinds of absence.

      An absent row is not a predicted deletion. An omitted column is unassessed, rather than treated as an explicitly blank value. Actual import behavior needs separate review.

      03

      Read the coverage.

      Images, inventory, tax, shipping, market prices and metafields are outside this comparison. Zero changes would not mean a complete check or a safe import.
