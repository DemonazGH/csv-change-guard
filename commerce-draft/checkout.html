A downloadable utility for local, read-only comparison of a supported Shopify product-CSV subset.

In the proposed beta package

  • A standalone HTML app for local comparisons; no CSV uploads or store credentials.
  • Synthetic sample files and a built-in demo to learn the workflow.
  • A quick start, field-coverage guide, and troubleshooting help.
  • HTML, CSV, and JSON report exports; unlimited comparisons within the documented limits.
  • A proposed one-business license, including staff and contractors working solely on that business’s own catalogs.
  • Proposed email support and maintenance updates when released for 12 months from purchase, without automatic renewal.

Check the fit first

Both files must be UTF-8, comma-delimited CSV from the same store and currency, with recognized handle, Title, and SKU columns. Matching uses exact, case-sensitive handle + non-empty SKU pairs.

Limits per file: 5,000 data records, 10 MiB, and 200 columns. Prices use the documented non-negative dot-decimal format.

Images, inventory, shipping, taxes, markets, metafields, and full importer behavior are unassessed. Missing or ambiguous identifiers mean incomplete comparison. Absent rows do not predict deletions; omitted columns differ from explicit blanks.

A report does not approve an import or guarantee safety. This utility does not perform imports or change a store.

Use and updates

One non-exclusive license for one business and its staff and contractors working solely on that business’s own catalogs. Use across that business’s owned stores and devices and ordinary backup copies would be allowed. Agency or multi-client services for separate businesses, resale, sublicensing, and redistribution are excluded. Reports may be shared for the business’s own purposes, subject to its authority over the data.

The delivered version may be kept and used without a comparison quota, within the documented limits. Email support and maintenance updates when released are proposed for 12 months from purchase through the original delivery channel, with no automatic renewal. There is no automatic updater; future major versions, feature dates, and perpetual support are not promised. Mandatory rights and any required updates remain unaffected.

Support

Contact csvchangeguard.support@gmail.com. Send error text or synthetic examples first; do not send catalog files, reports, credentials, or payment details. Custom onboarding, managed comparisons, and live-store access are excluded.
