# Support and privacy operations — current prelaunch procedure

**22 October 2026:** the owner explicitly approved keeping routine conversations
for about three months after closure, followed by a monthly review/deletion of
eligible mail. The procedure is manual; the owner performs future reviews. No
existing mail was deleted and no automation or account rule was enabled.
Domain and paid email are postponed. Use the [current website/Gmail notice](CURRENT_WEBSITE_GMAIL_PRIVACY.md)
and [current setup record](CURRENT_PRIVACY_SETUP.md). The separate
[purchase privacy review](PRIVACY_AND_CONSENT_REVIEW.md) remains a release gate;
this procedure does not activate sales or clear every legal/provider question.

## Minimise data and access

The owner alone handles the support/rights inbox. Ask for a short problem
description, app/browser version and a synthetic example. When purchase access
needs checking, use the minimum order reference/contact details. No customer
catalogues, reports, store credentials, full card data or identity documents by
default. Do not send real messages/attachments to assistants, external issue
trackers or Git. No production assistant delegation is proposed.

If a private merchant file arrives accidentally, do not open, forward, paste or
upload it to investigate. The owner handles the minimum necessary reply and
review/deletion in the mailbox. Record a content-free handling note if needed.
An incident involving disclosure requires an owner assessment under applicable
rules; do not promise every attachment was never processed by the mail provider.

Keep only the provider accounts and processing arrangements needed for website,
mail and checkout. No marketing lists, campaigns, analytics tags, CSV uploads or
bulk purchaser exports. A Lemon Squeezy purchaser subscriber record is not treated
as marketing consent. Keep requested support and essential service messages
separate from promotion.

## Adopted routine retention rule and separate record classes

| Record | Owner action / remaining decision |
|---|---|
| Routine enquiry/support thread | Mark closure date after resolution/no further action. At a monthly review, start deletion of eligible identifiable threads once 90 days have elapsed after closure. This creates a practical 90–121-day window before deletion starts, not an exact 90-day purge |
| Open complaint, purchase/refund dispute or legal hold | Retain only necessary evidence while the duty/claim applies. Note the reason, responsible owner and next review date. Reassess at closure; do not label every thread a permanent exception |
| Technical learning | Keep an anonymised issue description if useful. Removing a name alone does not anonymise a catalogue, screenshot or unique order identifier |
| Offline licence/support entitlement | No new customer database is planned. Use minimum provider order information for support, without promising provider deletion. The delivered licence continues independently of an online account. Decide any supplier-held entitlement record schedule before paid release |
| Accounting/tax documents | Accountant identifies which supplier records, periods and starting events apply. Keep these separately from routine support; do not assign every message a generic five-year period |
| Rights requests | Keep the minimum request/action evidence justified by the actual duty or claim. Confirm the review period; do not retain unnecessary identity documents |
| Website/checkout provider technical records | Obtain the applicable provider schedule and control limits. Do not invent a fixed Pages or merchant-of-record deletion date |

Gmail deletion normally moves messages to Trash for up to 30 further days.
Consumer Gmail's subsequent provider deletion, backups and legal/security
exceptions follow [Google's consumer retention information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention?hl=en).
The business CDPA's deletion periods are **not** terms of the current consumer
inbox. The notice must describe the actual stages rather than promise every
system copy disappears on day 90. No Trash purging or security/privacy
configuration was performed here.

The monthly review is an **owner procedure**, not an automation already running.
Use a minimal private register outside the checkout/AI-accessible workspace:
thread reference, closure/due date, exception reason and next review/action.
Do not copy message bodies, addresses or customer files into this repository.

## Rights and complaints workflow

1. Acknowledge the enquiry and record receipt/deadline in the owner's private
   register. Identify the controller and data flow concerned. GDPR responses are
   generally due within one month; assess lawful extensions and notify within
   that first month when applicable.
2. Verify identity proportionately using information already needed for the
   enquiry; do not ask for a passport by default. Avoid disclosing another
   person's order or mailbox data.
3. Search only necessary supplier-held records. Coordinate processor assistance
   under the applicable terms. Explain how to contact providers for data they
   control independently, including MoR duties.
4. Assess the requested right and any lawful retention exception. Reply with
   action, reason/limits and applicable complaint route, including UODO. Ordinary
   product-support response targets do not replace statutory deadlines.
5. Record the minimum outcome and review its retention. Do not claim an email
   deletion request erases tax records, third-party data or a buyer's offline ZIP.
