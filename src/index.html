Coverage, matching rules, and limitations

Exports include all findings, regardless of filters. Reports may contain sensitive values from your files. CSV formula-like cells are prefixed for safer spreadsheet viewing; JSON preserves original text.

WHAT THIS VERSION CHECKS

Small scope. Explicit rules.

Price movements and zero-price changes; blanked descriptions and other supported product fields; option changes; duplicate keys; unmatched rows; and selected format/dependency checks.

Uncertain matches are excluded rather than guessed. A missing row is not labelled as a deletion. Blank continuation cells are not treated as new product descriptions.

LOCAL BY DESIGN

Your files stay in your browser.

No analytics, model APIs, cookies, accounts, or file uploads. The prototype has no network calls or local-storage persistence. Clear the files or close the tab when finished.

It does not connect to Shopify, make a backup, validate the live store, or perform an import. A clean report is not a guarantee of a safe import.

NOT A FULL IMPORT SIMULATOR

Know what is outside the check.

Images, inventory, taxes, shipping, metafields, market prices, and unrecognized columns are not compared. SKU-less variants, arbitrary supplier formats, XLSX, and semicolon-delimited files are outside this prototype.

No payments are enabled. This is a testable product foundation—not a launched commercial service.
