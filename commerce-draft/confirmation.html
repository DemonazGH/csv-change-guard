Your local review starts with a download.

After a verified purchase, the payment provider would deliver the package and receipt. This preview has no connected order or delivery link.

Download and get started

  1. Save the ZIP from your purchase delivery.

    Keep your receipt and download instructions. Do not send your catalog files to the seller or payment provider.

  2. Extract the complete package.

    On Windows, use Extract All. Keep the included help, samples, and app together.

  3. Open the app and try the built-in demo.

    Open dist/index.html in your browser. The demo uses synthetic data and lets you learn the report before choosing your own files.

  4. Review the documented scope.

    Only choose authorized files that fit the supported format and identifiers. A comparison report is not an approval to import.

A real download is supplied only through the configured purchase-delivery flow. No entitlement check or payment integration is implemented here.

Sample failure-state copy

If purchase or delivery does not complete

Checkout was cancelled

No completed order has been confirmed in this sample flow. Return to the product page when you are ready. Before paying again, check whether the provider has already sent you a receipt.

Activation note: the provider must supply the actual cancellation status; this draft cannot verify payment state.

Payment is still being confirmed

Please wait for the provider’s confirmation and check your purchase email. Avoid repeating the purchase while its status is unclear. If confirmation does not arrive, contact csvchangeguard.support@gmail.com with the order number and purchase email.

Never include card details, catalog files, reports, or store credentials in a support message.

Your download link is unavailable

Check the provider’s original receipt and delivery message. If the link is missing or expired, contact csvchangeguard.support@gmail.com with the order number and purchase email so delivery can be checked.

Owner review: confirm the provider’s resend/recovery process before using this copy in a live flow. Test mode blocks file downloads and did not validate recovery.

The app will not open on your device

Extract the ZIP first, then open the included quick start. Some managed browsers block local HTML; an optional localhost fallback is provided. Keep browser sandboxing enabled and respect your device policy.

If both routes are blocked, contact csvchangeguard.support@gmail.com with the browser version and error text, using a synthetic example if needed. A support diagnosis is not a condition of the proposed voluntary refund.

Support, updates, and refunds

Proposed email support and maintenance updates when released last 12 months from purchase, with no automatic renewal. The first-response target is two business days, Monday–Friday excluding Polish public holidays; resolution times are not guaranteed. Keep using the delivered version. Mandatory rights and required updates are unaffected.

Proposed full voluntary refund: email csvchangeguard.support@gmail.com within 14 calendar days of purchase with your order number and purchase email. No reason is required, including after download. Refunds would be handled through Lemon Squeezy; payment-provider and bank timing may vary. This is separate from mandatory consumer rights.

After a completed full refund, stop using and delete the utility copies, including backups; your own generated reports may be kept. There is no remote revocation. These proposals are not effective purchase terms. See the full draft terms.
