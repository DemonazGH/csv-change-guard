## Proposed customer policy

We offer a full refund if you contact **csvchangeguard.support@gmail.com**
within 14 calendar days of purchase. No reason is required, including if you
have downloaded or tried the software. Include your order number and purchase
email so we can locate the payment. Troubleshooting is not a condition of a
timely request. Do not send merchant CSVs, reports, card details or credentials.

Requests sent by the end of the 14th calendar day after the purchase date
qualify. We use Europe/Warsaw time for this voluntary promise and do not shorten
any longer deadline provided by mandatory law.

We will approve timely requests under this promise and arrange a full refund
through Lemon Squeezy, the merchant of record, without deducting our platform
or support costs. The provider and payment method control when the credit
appears on your statement. We aim to acknowledge email within two business days,
Monday-Friday excluding Polish public holidays; this target does not replace
any applicable legal deadline.

If the download is missing, corrupt or the product does not work as described,
contact us even after that voluntary window. You can also use the payment/order
help route in your Lemon Squeezy receipt. We will help with access or address
the complaint and applicable remedies. The voluntary window does not limit
mandatory rights concerning withdrawal, undelivered, faulty or misdescribed
digital content, required updates, price reduction or refunds. A commercial-use
description does not itself remove any protected buyer rights.

We do not treat a download alone as a blanket waiver of withdrawal rights.
Any legally relevant immediate-delivery consent, acknowledgement and confirmation
must be handled by the applicable purchase process. Our voluntary promise above
applies even after download.

After a completed full refund, stop using and delete the utility copies covered
by that purchase, including backups. You may keep your own reports and business
data. The utility has no remote-revocation mechanism; processing a refund does
not technically erase a local copy.
