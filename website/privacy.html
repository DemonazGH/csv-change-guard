Who handles your information

DZMITRY SALAVEI, Poland, provides CSV Change Guard and is responsible for the website and enquiry handling described here. Contact the owner for support or privacy questions at csvchangeguard.support@gmail.com.

The utility keeps your files local

The downloadable utility reads selected CSV files and creates reports in your browser. It does not upload catalogues, connect to your store or call AI services. The public demo uses the same comparison engine with fixed synthetic inputs only, processed in your browser. It cannot select or read your files. The website has no file upload or contact form. Please do not email merchant CSVs, reports, passwords, store credentials or payment details. Use a short description and synthetic examples for support.

Website visits and enquiries

Cloudflare hosts and protects the website. It receives technical request information such as IP addresses, browser/request metadata and traffic data to serve the page and protect its network. We do not use advertising scripts or implement a separate visitor database or routine export of visitor logs. Hosting still involves technical processing; these statements are not a promise that no provider cookies or network records can ever exist.

Optional website analytics

The home page, Shopify CSV price-change guide and this privacy page offer optional Cloudflare Web Analytics. The analytics script does not load until you select Allow website analytics. Selecting Decline, leaving the choice unanswered or disabling JavaScript keeps these analytics off. You can still read the pages, use the synthetic demo and download the free beta.

If you allow analytics, Cloudflare receives page-view and page-load performance measurements and reports aggregate information such as page paths, referring sites, country, browser and device type. We use these statistics to understand how people find the website and improve its information and performance. The beacon uses a fresh page-load reference to relate measurements for the current page; it is not a persistent visitor identifier. Cloudflare's beacon documentation also describes page and referring URLs and in-memory timing and memory metrics. Cloudflare says its analytics does not use cookies or localStorage, track people across sites, or identify visitors by fingerprinting. It removes IP addresses before storing analytics data and says query strings are not logged in Web Analytics. Technical requests still reach Cloudflare with IP addresses and request metadata as described above. See its analytics explanation, available dimensions and analytics FAQ.

Our website stores only your analytics choice in the browser tab's sessionStorage, without a visitor identifier. This remembers the choice within that tab's browsing session; browser session-restoration behavior may affect how long it remains. Select Change analytics choice in the footer to change or withdraw your choice. Withdrawal reloads the page with analytics disabled; measurements already sent are not recalled. Where GDPR applies, optional analytics relies on your consent, which you can withdraw without affecting processing before withdrawal.

No analytics runs in the public synthetic demo, downloaded utility, free-use terms or saved reports. CSV contents, filenames, findings and offline activity are not included in website analytics. We do not collect custom click events, count completed ZIP downloads or measure individual or repeat app users. Website visits are not proof that someone used the app or found it useful.

Cloudflare's weekly report sends the owner aggregate website visits, page views and page-load statistics at the existing support Gmail address. A separate Cloudflare relay sends selected aggregate statistics to the owner's private Telegram chat: account-wide visits and page views when available, with the reporting period and number of websites where supported. Unavailable figures are labelled as unavailable. The Telegram summary does not include page paths, referrers, performance details, CSV files, saved comparison reports, support messages or raw visitor records. These are owner notifications, not a visitor mailing list, marketing email or Telegram support channel.

Enquiries and feedback

When you email us, we use your email address, name if supplied, message and any app/browser details you choose to include to answer the enquiry and troubleshoot. Contacting support is voluntary, but we need a reply address and enough detail to understand your request. We do not use enquiries to create marketing lists or advertising audiences, and this website has no newsletter signup.

The free beta ZIP and its terms can be downloaded without an account, email address or payment. Cloudflare handles the technical download request as part of website hosting; downloading does not send us your CSVs or reports. The browser trial is also available immediately with supplied synthetic examples. Email feedback is optional. We use any general catalog role, browser information and comments you choose to send to understand the feedback and answer you. This correspondence follows the same support-inbox retention procedure below. There is no separate recruitment database or mailing list, and sending feedback does not subscribe you to marketing.

Where GDPR applies, our basis for website operation and ordinary enquiries is our legitimate interests in providing information about the product, keeping the website reliable and secure, and answering requests people choose to send. We consider those interests alongside individuals' rights. Handling an applicable privacy request or other statutory duty may require processing under the relevant legal obligation. Necessary evidence for a complaint or legal claim may be used for our legitimate interests in resolving or defending that matter.

Providers and international processing

Cloudflare processes information to deliver and protect the site and provide the optional analytics described above. Its roles depend on the purpose: it processes covered customer content/logs on a customer's behalf and also processes network-related information for its own purposes. See its privacy policy and customer DPA.

Consumer Gmail handles our support correspondence under Google's consumer terms and privacy policy. Google processes message content and related service information under those documents, including automated service/security features and optional features according to account settings. Google states that it does not offer a DPA or act as our processor for consumer Gmail. This is the current consumer service, not an activated Workspace business mailbox. See Google's explanation.

Telegram handles the owner's operational bot messages and related service information under its privacy policy. Telegram describes itself as the controller for personal data associated with use of its service. Cloudflare stores the bot credentials and the owner's private chat identifier as encrypted operational settings to route notifications; this identifier does not identify website visitors. The bot channel is ordinary cloud messaging, not an end-to-end encrypted Secret Chat. Telegram's policy describes its storage, retention and international group-company processing arrangements; we do not promise EEA-only processing or a separate Telegram processing agreement.

Provider processing may take place outside Poland and the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Google's transfer-framework information and Cloudflare's privacy policy/DPA explain applicable adequacy, Data Privacy Framework and contractual safeguards, where relevant. These are provider arrangements; we do not promise EEA-only processing or claim that we signed a separate Gmail processing agreement. Contact the owner for information about the relevant safeguards or how to obtain a copy.

The owner handles support and privacy requests. We do not routinely forward enquiries to other mailboxes, publish their contents, or send customer messages and attachments to AI assistants or public issue trackers. Providers may process information for their own purposes described in their policies. Information may also need to be disclosed where an applicable legal duty requires it.

How long we keep information

For ordinary support enquiries, our rule is to keep a conversation for about three months after it is finished, then review closed conversations once a month and delete eligible ones once 90 days have passed. Deletion normally starts within 90–121 days after closure. This is a manual owner procedure, not an automatic deletion rule.

Gmail normally keeps deleted messages in Trash for up to another 30 days. Google's subsequent deletion, backup and legal/security retention processes are separate; deleting a message is not a promise of immediate erasure from every provider system. See Gmail deletion and Google's retention explanation.

If a conversation concerns an unresolved complaint, a legal claim or a record we must retain by law, we keep only the necessary evidence while that matter remains open and for any applicable retention/claim period afterward. We review the reason at closure and during subsequent retention reviews. This does not give every email an indefinite exception. Useful technical lessons may be kept after removing information that could identify a person or their catalogue.

Cloudflare applies its purpose-based retention criteria for provider-held technical records, described in its privacy policy. We do not set a separate fixed deadline for all of Cloudflare's own network or legally required records.

Cloudflare currently makes Web Analytics data available for the previous six months. Its FAQ describes seven days of unsampled beacon data followed by a sampled, aggregated dataset; dashboard queries can also use sampling. This service-access window is not a promise that every provider record is erased after six months. We do not routinely export raw analytics or build individual visitor histories. Weekly aggregate report emails use the same monthly review procedure: eligible reports are reviewed for deletion about three months after receipt, normally within 90–121 days. This is not automatic deletion. Gmail Trash and provider deletion/backup processes remain separate.

No automatic expiry is configured for the owner's Telegram bot history. Its messages remain in the chat unless deleted; Telegram's service retention and deletion processes are separate from the Gmail procedure above. The relay does not create a separate report archive.

Your rights

Where GDPR applies, you may request access, correction, erasure or restriction, and portability where its conditions apply. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. If a separate activity relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. Rights have conditions, including duties to retain particular records.

Email csvchangeguard.support@gmail.com. We use proportionate checks to verify a request and do not ask for identity documents by default. The normal GDPR response period is one month; lawful extensions for complex or numerous requests require notice within that first month. For information a provider controls independently, its privacy contact may also be relevant.

You may complain to Poland's supervisory authority, UODO, or another competent supervisory authority. The owner does not make solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about people using support. The utility's comparison findings are reports for human review; Google and Cloudflare explain their own automated processing separately.

This notice explains the current website and consumer inbox; it is not a legal-compliance certificate or a purchase-policy approval. Local CSV processing does not remove the separate privacy responsibilities described here.
