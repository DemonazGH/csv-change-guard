  - article:
    - generic: WHAT THIS VERSION CHECKS
    - heading "Small scope. Explicit rules." [level=3]
    - paragraph: Price movements and zero-price changes; blanked descriptions and other supported product fields; option changes; duplicate keys; unmatched rows; and selected format/dependency checks.
    - paragraph: Uncertain matches are excluded rather than guessed. A missing row is not labelled as a deletion. Blank continuation cells are not treated as new product descriptions.
  - article:
    - generic: LOCAL BY DESIGN
    - heading "Your files stay in your browser." [level=3]
    - paragraph: No analytics, model APIs, cookies, accounts, or file uploads. The prototype has no network calls or local-storage persistence. Clear the files or close the tab when finished.
    - paragraph: It does not connect to Shopify, make a backup, validate the live store, or perform an import. A clean report is not a guarantee of a safe import.
  - article:
    - generic: NOT A FULL IMPORT SIMULATOR
    - heading "Know what is outside the check." [level=3]
    - paragraph: Images, inventory, taxes, shipping, metafields, market prices, and unrecognized columns are not compared. SKU-less variants, arbitrary supplier formats, XLSX, and semicolon-delimited files are outside this prototype.
    - paragraph: No payments are enabled. This is a testable product foundation—not a launched commercial service.
