## Your CSVs and reports stay on your computer

The downloadable utility reads and compares your selected CSV files in your
browser. It has no upload endpoint, store connection, analytics, cookies or
application storage. We do not receive the selected CSV contents or generated
reports through the utility. Clearing files removes the app's references and
visible results; it is not a guarantee of secure memory or disk erasure.
Your browser, extensions, device, backups and saved reports remain under your
control. Do not email us merchant CSVs, reports, passwords or payment details;
use synthetic examples for support.

## Your rights and contact

Where GDPR applies, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction and,
where its conditions apply, portability. You may object to processing based on
legitimate interests. If a separate activity relies on consent, you may withdraw
that consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. Necessary purchase
processing is not based on a blanket privacy-consent checkbox.

Contact **[production privacy/support email]**. We will use the minimum necessary
checks to verify a request and normally respond within one month; the law allows
an extension for complex or numerous requests, with notice within that first
month. Rights have conditions, including obligations to retain certain records.
You may complain to Poland's supervisory authority,
[UODO](https://uodo.gov.pl/), or another competent supervisory authority. For data
Lemon Squeezy or another provider controls independently, its privacy contact is
also available; we will help identify the appropriate route.
